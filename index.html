Method & assumptions

Trajectory. 3-DOF point-mass, RK4 at 0.5 ms steps, integrated along the line of sight. Drag from the standard G7 or G1 table: a = ρ·v²·Cd(M)·π / (8·BC), with BC converted to kg/m² (×703.07). Wind, head/tail wind and slope are integrated directly; no lag-time or Rifleman's-rule shortcuts.

Atmosphere. ICAO standard pressure for the altitude unless you enter station pressure. Air density includes humidity (Arden Buck vapour pressure). Speed of sound = √(1.4·R·T). Zero angle is solved at 100 m (or the set zero) on the level in the current atmosphere, so a dialled zero is assumed confirmed in similar conditions.

Spin and jump. Spin drift uses Litz's fit, SD = 1.25·(Sg + 1.2)·TOF1.83 (inches), added to windage, rightward for right-hand twist. Vertical aerodynamic jump from crosswind uses Litz's 0.01·Sg − 0.0024·L + 0.032 MOA per mph; right-hand twist, wind from the left prints high. Sg is Miller's formula with velocity and atmosphere corrections. Bullet lengths for the ELD-X, Gamehead and V-MAX are estimates; they only affect Sg, spin drift and jump.

Units. True milliradians (1 000 per radian; 1 mrad = 10 cm at 100 m). MOA is true minutes of angle (2.91 cm at 100 m). Clicks use 0.1 mrad or ¼ MOA.

Card agreement. Zeiss LRP S3 and Arken EPL-4 turrets are true mrad (confirmed 22 Oct 2026). The v1.0/v1.1 cards were 1.86 % high (6400-mil units) and are superseded by the v2.0 cards, which come from this solver and match it. Remaining checks are MV/BC truing from observed drops and scope tracking (tall-target test).
